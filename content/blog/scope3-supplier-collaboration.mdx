## The Scope 3 Crisis

Your chief sustainability officer sends an email to the procurement team:

> "We're committing to net-zero by 2030. For our SBTi submission, we need Scope 3 emissions data from our top 100 suppliers. Can you collect this by end of quarter?"

Two weeks later, procurement reports back:

> "We emailed 100 suppliers. 15 responded. 8 said 'we don't track emissions.' 4 sent partial data. 83 didn't reply."

**Result:** Your Scope 3 estimate is based on generic industry averages for 83% of your supplier base. Your sustainability team spends 3 months chasing spreadsheets. SBTi validation gets delayed.

This is every large company's Scope 3 nightmare.

## Why Suppliers Don't Respond

<Callout type="critical">
**Fact:** 80% of suppliers don't track or report emissions. Not because they don't care, but because:
1. They don't know who's asking or why
2. They get 5+ carbon data requests per year (different customers)
3. Email gets lost
4. Data entry takes 2 hours for unclear benefit
5. They don't have the systems to track scope 3 of their own operations
</Callout>

Competitors address this with supplier portals. You can create a form, invite suppliers via email, and wait for responses.

**Result:** Same low response rate (15–20%) because suppliers still have to:
- Click email link
- Create login
- Fill form
- Find data
- Remember to submit

Every friction point drops response rate by 20%.

## Supplier Performance Analytics

Not all suppliers respond equally. CarbonSite tracks:

- **Response rate:** Who submits on time
- **Data completeness:** Are they providing all required fields
- **Data quality score:** Are values realistic or suspicious
- **Trend:** Is supplier improving or declining

You can segment suppliers by performance:
- **Top performers:** Respond early, complete data, high quality
- **Average:** Respond eventually, partial data, need reminders
- **Laggards:** Never respond, require phone follow-ups

## Handling Unresponsive Suppliers

For suppliers who never respond, you have options:

1. **Use industry average** (most conservative approach for SBTi)
2. **Spend-based estimation** (CarbonSite ML model trained on responsive suppliers)
3. **Peer benchmark** (average emissions intensity of similar suppliers in same industry)

All methods are defensible in SBTi validation + external audits. Transparency is key: document why you chose each method.
