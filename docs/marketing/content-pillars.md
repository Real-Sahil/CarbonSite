## Measurement & Reporting

**Monthly Metrics:**
- Organic traffic by post (Google Analytics)
- Search rankings for target keywords (Search Console)
- Blog engagement: time on page, scroll depth, CTA clicks
- Conversion: blog reader → pricing page → trial signup

**Quarterly Review:**
- Top-performing posts (repurpose as guides, case studies)
- Low-performing posts (revise, re-promote, or sunset)
- Emerging keywords (update existing posts, create new content)
- Competitor blog performance (identify gaps, respond)

**Success Criteria (6 months):**
- 50+ keywords ranking top 10 in Google
- 10k+ monthly organic blog traffic
- 5%+ blog reader to pricing page conversion
- 2+ customer conversations traced to blog post
