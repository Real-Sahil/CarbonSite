# Marketing site

Moved word for word out of CLAUDE.md so it is read only when working in this area (``app/(marketing)/`, `components/marketing/``). CLAUDE.md keeps the rules that apply everywhere and an index of these files.

### Marketing site (`app/(marketing)`)
- **One system:** build pages from `components/marketing/kit.tsx` (Section tones dark/paper/light, H1/H2/Lead, `ButtonLink` primary then secondary, then `TextLink`, `ProductShot`/`ProductLoop`, `ClosingCta`). Tokens are the `--color-mk-*` block at the top of `app/globals.css`: graphite ink, cool off-white, one ember accent (`mk-accent` fills with white text, `mk-accent-lit` for text on dark). Newsreader for display (H1/H2), Inter for body, Geist Mono loaded alongside (`app/layout.tsx`). Long-form text uses `.mk-prose`; legal pages use `LegalShell`. Nav groups live in `NAV` in `site-nav.tsx`. The header is a flat full-width bar in the Vercel style: ink over the dark hero, paper once the page scrolls. Top-level entries are plain links; a group opens a panel of headed columns with link text only (no descriptions or promo cards). Sign in is a text link beside a solid primary CTA. Below 1024px the menu is one panel with every group expanded. The sitemap lists pages by hand in `app/sitemap.ts`.
- **Only real product and real claims:** screenshots and background loops in `public/marketing/{screens,loops}` are captured from the local demo tenant (Northgate Civils Ltd, fictional, labelled "demo data") with `scripts/marketing/record-loops.mjs`; activity comes from `scripts/marketing/demo-activity-csv.py` through the normal import and calculation pipeline. No invented testimonials, customer metrics, competitor comparisons or statistics. Every factor value, feature and plan limit quoted must match the code (plan copy follows `PLAN_FEATURES`/`PLAN_LIMITS`).
- **Demo analytics** (`components/marketing/demo-analytics.tsx`, home page and `/product#analytics`): the demo tenant's FY2025 totals, scope split and seven largest categories, copied from its dashboard and analytics captures. The scope split is a doughnut and the categories are bars; both draw in when scrolled into view, and the total counts up (no transition or count under reduced motion). Update the constants whenever the screenshots are recaptured; never add a figure the demo tenant does not show.
- **Scroll and pointer effects** (`components/marketing/string-tune.tsx`, `@fiddle-digital/string-tune` 1.2.6, MIT, mounted in the marketing layout; the library's docs site is blocked from agent sessions, so what is known about its `string` attributes came from reading the package): String Tune's smooth-scroll mode stays off because Lenis smooths the wheel app-wide (`components/scroll-provider.tsx`, mounted in the root layout; not running under reduced motion, and switching that setting stops or starts it). Its parallax module only runs in that mode, so the background drift is `--progress` in CSS. Modules: `StringProgress` (the video backgrounds drift), `StringSplit` (plain-string `H2`s reveal word by word, `aria-label` keeps the text readable), `StringMagnetic` (primary `ButtonLink`), `StringTilt` (`ProductFrame`, 3 degrees), the last two only with a precise pointer. `Reveal` and `SectionIntro` fade content up when it first enters view; `reveal(i, "self"|"inner", cols)` (kit.tsx) spreads the same effect, staggered by place in a row, onto card elements in the grids on the home, product, pricing, security, methodology, field app, resources and solution pages ("inner" fades the card's children so hairline-gap grids never show the gap colour) (an empty `string=""` key only marks the element `-inview`). **Safe by construction:** every rule in `globals.css` sits under `.st-on`, which the script adds only after the library has started and only when the visitor has not asked for reduced motion; reveal hiding also needs the library's own `string-inited` mark, and a scroll sweep marks elements scrolled past unseen so nothing stays hidden. The hero `H1` types itself: `H1` in `kit.tsx` wraps each character in an `aria-hidden` span, timed in CSS by `.mk-type-char` in `globals.css`, outside `.st-on` so it does not wait for the library. A visually hidden copy carries the full title; reduced motion and print show the whole title at once, with no caret. The caret sits on Newsreader's cap height (`top` 0.053em and `height` 0.684em, both measured on `H` with canvas `measureText`). Splitting costs about 1% of the title's width (1.11% at 76 px on desktop, 1.13% at 44 px on mobile, 0.77% on the product page), because kerning and ligatures do not run across span boundaries. The text is in the server HTML, but each character is its own element, so re-measure LCP when changing it. Keep other effects off the first screen. Licence note in `docs/THIRD_PARTY_SOURCES.md`.
- **What the marketing pages say about the calculation:** outcomes and verifiability, not the recipe. Public: the standards followed, factor sources and licences, traceability, immutable published figures, that totals carry an uncertainty range, and that rules are versioned. Kept in the app and the assurance pack: factor-scoring and fallback order, regional grid rules, hint matching, forecasting model names and selection, any accuracy or error figure, technique names (no "Monte Carlo" on marketing pages). The methodology page shows each version's `summary` (plain language); `changes` is the full account for customers and auditors. Never publish forecast accuracy.
- **Demo labelling:** the only caption under demo material is `*Demo data` (`DemoNote`, rendered by every `ProductFrame` and under the platform film); frames carry no window title and no other caption, so do not add a caption prop back.
- **Chart colours:** one scope palette, `SCOPE_COLORS` in `components/charts/palette.ts` = `--color-scope-1..3` (teal `#0B8F80`, ember `#E0692A`, blue `#3B6FD4`), checked with the dataviz palette validator on light and dark. App charts and marketing use it; the PDF report templates still carry their own print palette.
- **Platform film** (home page hero, `components/marketing/launch-film.tsx`, 90 s, source `videos/src/videos/platform/`; replaced the 56 s launch film, whose composition `videos/src/videos/launch/` remains for the Launch composition): built in code in `videos/` (Remotion, its own npm package, excluded from the app's tsconfig, lint and tests; `videos/BRAND.md` holds its rules). Its product screens are redrawn from the demo tenant screenshots with the same tokens, not captured, and every figure is the demo tenant's (labelled demo data). The hero plays `public/marketing/film/platform-720.webm` muted with an MP4 fallback; "Play with sound" swaps to the 1080p cut (WebM with Opus, MP4 fallback) inside the click so the browser allows audio. The score is original (`videos/scripts/compose.py`). Re-render with `cd videos && pnpm run setup && pnpm run setup:platform && bun scripts/render.ts Platform metricora-platform --duration 90 --poster 85`, then copy the files into `public/marketing/film/` under the `platform-` names the component reads (720p30 WebM and MP4, 1080p60 sound WebM and MP4, poster). The web files in the repo were encoded from the 1080p60 review render with ffmpeg (VP9 CRF 36 and 34, H.264 CRF 26 and 23).
- **Report types:** every type with a handler in `lib/reports/registry.ts` (`hasTypedTemplate()`) renders its own HTML layout through headless Chromium; if Chromium fails, `lib/reports/worker.ts` falls back to the generic PDFKit report from the handler's `pdfkitData` and logs a warning. `inventory` has no template and is always PDFKit. Template figures come from the run (library, methodology, GWP) and org records (base year, SBTi target); SECR energy is computed from the run's records by `secrEnergyFromCalculations()` (`lib/reports/secr-energy.ts`, litres at DESNZ 2025 gross CV) unless the form supplies it.
- **Field app availability:** Android is live on Google Play (`app.metricora.metricora_mobile`, `PLAY_URL`). The iPhone app is live on the App Store (`APP_STORE_URL`, `https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/metricora/id6811462209`, bundle `app.metricora.metricoraMobile`), approved in October 2026. Both store badges are `GooglePlayBadge` and `AppStoreBadge` in `components/marketing/brand-marks.tsx`, shown in the site footer and on `/field-app`; `sameAs` on the home page JSON-LD lists both listings. The iOS app is iPhone-only (`TARGETED_DEVICE_FAMILY = 1`), so App Store Connect asks for iPhone screenshots only; they must be raw iOS captures (no Android bars, no device frame, no wallpaper) at 1290x2796, from the demo tenant, never real customer or project names.
- Removed pages (comparison, case studies, `/calculation`, the old blog posts) redirect in `next.config.ts`.
- **Display serif:** headings use Newsreader through `--font-display` (`app/globals.css`, loaded in `app/layout.tsx`). That is a brand choice the owner makes, not an open bug. The one-line swap is `--font-display` at `app/globals.css:115`, to `var(--font-sans)` for Inter or another loaded face. The hero caret is measured on Newsreader's cap height (`top` 0.053em, `height` 0.684em), so re-measure it after any swap, and drop the Newsreader import from `app/layout.tsx` if the face goes.
- **Plain titles on blog posts and legal pages:** `app/(marketing)/blog/[slug]/page.tsx` and `components/marketing/legal-shell.tsx` use a plain `h1`, not the typed `H1` from `kit.tsx`. The typing effect is for one hero title per page. Long article and policy titles would otherwise type out letter by letter, with a span per character. Keep it that way unless a page is meant to type its title.
